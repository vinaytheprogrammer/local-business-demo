# Apex Pro Local Business Website & Growth Packages Demo

A complete, production-ready 3-tier local service business website and automation package designed for local contractors, trades, and home services.

## Packages Included

- **Package 1 (Starter):** 1-3 page mobile-friendly website, basic Google Business Profile setup, Google Search Console, sitemap, quote request form sending to email.
- **Package 2 (Growth):** 4-5 static pages, full Google Business Profile optimization (services, Q&A, starter posts), GA4 analytics, on-page SEO.
- **Package 3 (Pro):** 5 pages + 2 high-intent landing pages (Emergency & Installations), dynamic Google Review QR code generator, 3 local citations, quote form leads routed to Google Sheets + WhatsApp instant alert.
