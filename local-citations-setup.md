# Three Local Directory Citations Setup Guide (Package 3)

Local directory citations are mentions of your business **Name, Address, and Phone Number (NAP)** on high domain-authority business directories. Consistent citations validate your geographic location to Google's ranking algorithms.

---

## 1. Golden Rule: 100% NAP Consistency

To prevent Google algorithmic confusion, your NAP format must match across all 3 directories:

| Parameter | Exact Canonical Format |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Business Name** | Apex Pro Home Services |
| **Street Address** | 742 Evergreen Terrace |
| **City, State, Zip** | Springfield, IL 62704 |
| **Primary Phone** | (800) 555-0199 |
| **Website URL** | `https://apexproservices.com` |

---

## 2. Directory 1: Yelp for Business (Domain Authority: 93)

- **Claim Portal:** [biz.yelp.com](https://biz.yelp.com)
- **Primary Category:** Heating & Air Conditioning / HVAC
- **Secondary Categories:** Plumbing, Electricians
- **Listing Copy:**
  > "Apex Pro Home Services is Springfield's trusted local specialist for 24/7 heating, cooling, plumbing, and electrical repairs. Licensed technicians, upfront pricing, 60-minute emergency arrival window, and 100% satisfaction guarantee."
- **Key Attributes:**
  - Emergency Service: Yes
  - Accepts Credit Cards: Yes
  - Free Estimates: Yes
  - By Appointment Only: No (Walk-ins/Calls welcome)
- **Verification Method:** Instant Automated Phone Verification or SMS PIN.

---

## 3. Directory 2: The Real YellowPages (YP.com) (Domain Authority: 87)

- **Claim Portal:** [adsolutions.yp.com](https://adsolutions.yp.com)
- **Primary Category:** Air Conditioning Contractors & Systems
- **Secondary Categories:** Plumbers, Electric Contractors
- **Listing Copy:**
  > "Springfield residential and commercial HVAC, plumbing, and electrical services. Serving Sangamon County since 2011 with fully stocked trucks, emergency dispatch, and guaranteed workmanship."
- **Verification Method:** Phone Call to business line with automated verification code.

---

## 4. Directory 3: Bing Places for Business (Domain Authority: 94)

- **Claim Portal:** [bingplaces.com](https://www.bingplaces.com)
- **Primary Feature:** 1-Click Sync directly with Google Business Profile!
- **Category:** HVAC Contractor
- **Benefits:**
  - Powers Microsoft Copilot & Bing Maps local search queries
  - Inherits verified Google reviews, photos, hours, and descriptions automatically
- **Verification Method:** Automated sync via Google Account authentication in 60 seconds.

## 6. Citation Verification & Indexing Checklist

- [ ] All three profiles use exact identical phone number formatting: `(800) 555-0199`
- [ ] Website URL includes secure protocol `https://`
- [ ] Business operating hours match Google Business Profile
- [ ] At least 3 high-resolution branded photos uploaded per directory
- [ ] Profile URLs documented in client handover spreadsheet
